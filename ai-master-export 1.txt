## Bug Found

The critical issue is in **`0006_crm_tables.sql`**: the `clients` table has a `proposal_id` foreign key referencing `proposals(id)`, but `proposals` is created *after* `clients`. This creates a circular dependency that will fail on execution.

Here's the complete fixed code:
